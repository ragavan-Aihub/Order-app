## Privacy Policy — Farm2Flavours

**Last updated:** DATE

**Contact:** SUPPORT EMAIL · SUPPORT PHONE

### 1. Who we are

Farm2Flavours (“we”, “us”) operates the Farm2Flavour mobile app for customers in SERVICE AREA (e.g. India). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the app.

### 2. What we collect

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Mobile phone number** | Sign-in via one-time SMS code; account identity |
| **Name** | Delivery and order communication |
| **Delivery addresses** | Fulfil and deliver your orders (up to 3 saved addresses) |
| **Order details** | Products, quantities, totals, status, payment method choice (cash or UPI at delivery) |
| **Push notification token** | Send order status updates (and optional “notify me” when a product becomes available) |
| **App interactions** | Operate the service (e.g. sign-in events stored by our hosting provider) |

We **do not** collect payment card details in the app. Payment is made at delivery (cash or UPI).

We **do not** record your microphone or voice. The app does not use voice ordering in the version published on Google Play.

We **do not** sell your personal information.

### 3. How we use information

- Create and manage your account
- Process, deliver, and support orders
- Send push notifications about your orders (if you allow notifications)
- Comply with law and prevent abuse

### 4. Service providers

We use trusted processors who store or process data on our instructions:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, file storage (e.g. product images)
- **SMS provider** (2Factor.in) — send OTP codes to your phone
- **Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging)** — deliver push notifications on Android

They may process data in regions where they operate. We use them under their terms and our configuration.

### 5. Retention

We keep order and account information as long as needed to run the business, support you, and meet legal or accounting requirements. You may ask us to delete or correct your data subject to applicable law and ongoing orders.

### 6. Your choices

- **Notifications:** You can disable push notifications in Android settings.
- **Account / data requests:** Contact SUPPORT EMAIL.

### 7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

### 8. Security

We use industry-standard measures through our providers (encrypted connections, access controls). No method is 100% secure.

### 9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new date at the top. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

### 10. Contact

Questions about privacy: **SUPPORT EMAIL** · **SUPPORT PHONE**  
Postal / business address: BUSINESS ADDRESS (optional but recommended)

## Data safety (Play Console)

When filling Google Play **Data safety**, align with the policy above. Typical answers for the **customer** app:

| Question | Typical answer |
|----------|----------------|
| Data collected | Phone number, name, address, purchase history, app interactions, device or other IDs (push token) |
| Data shared | With service providers (hosting, SMS, push) — not sold |
| Optional vs required | Phone required for account; notifications optional |
| Encrypted in transit | Yes (HTTPS) |
| Users can request deletion | Yes (via contact email) |
| Audio / microphone | **No data collected** |
| Location | **No** precise GPS (addresses are typed by user) |

Re-check against the actual app before submitting; Play requires accuracy.
